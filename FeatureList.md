**Features**
- Custom UI and C++ engine built around a configurable component stack structure for endless combinations of effects and sources.
- Multiple spout input & outputs
- Supports multiple milkdrop layers with dynamic blend & mix controls. 
- Shader effects & filters, with the option of adding your own custom shaders.
- Full 4d particle system, ribbon trails, sprite effects, 3d Model support.
  - (You can draw a spout or milkdrop layer 100s/1000s of times with different blends/orientations/positions through the particle system to create new unique effects from your existing presets and sources)
- All time-controls BPM-linked with loads of Audio Reactive options (Most properties can be linked to FFT bass, mid, treble and custom ranges).
- Property envelopes ; link properties to BPM-linked sine-waves and custom shapes
- Midi & OSC controller in/out support. 
- Easily add your own graphic assets (images, 3d models, other sources) to your performace
- Endless combinations of composable, customisable effects on multiple controllable layers

Usable as an automated visualiser, imagery source for other software via spout, as a full VJ performance tool or just for the joy of messing about with all the effects and making the pretty patterns :).
